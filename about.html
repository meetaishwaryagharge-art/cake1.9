Our Journey

Varyzon Cake Shop

A Premium Bakery Brand

Varyzon Cake Shop is a premium, 100% pure vegetarian bakery brand founded by Chef Virendra Sawant, bringing over 25 years of professional experience in the Bakery & Pastry industry.

Our passion is to create fresh, delicious, beautifully designed cakes and desserts that make every celebration memorable.

We specialize in customized designer cakes, birthday and anniversary cakes, wedding cakes, premium pastries, tea-time cakes, handmade chocolates, desserts, and corporate gifting.

My Professional Journey

Chef Virendra Sawant began his professional journey in 1999 after completing his Diploma in Bakery & Pastry.

Throughout his career, he gained valuable experience working with prestigious five-star hotels, including:

  • Hyatt Regency, Mumbai
  • Le Royal Meridien, Mumbai
  • The Orchid, Mumbai

International Experience

His international experience includes working as a Head Baker on hospitality projects in Iraq and Afghanistan.

Our Entrepreneurial Journey

In 2012, Chef Virendra Sawant began his entrepreneurial journey with Occasion Cake Shop, successfully building and managing the bakery business until 2023.

With extensive industry knowledge, creativity, and a passion for excellence, he later established Varyzon Cake Shop, combining international baking expertise with innovative cake designs and personalized service.
